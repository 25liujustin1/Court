# LPC avatar credits

Avatar parts in `parts/` are cut from the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator) (Liberated Pixel Cup art). Each piece below is used under the first non-share-alike license it offers (CC0, OGA-BY 3.0 or CC-BY); the full list of licenses each artist offers is shown. Recolouring uses the generator's palettes. Built by `build-lpc.cjs`.

The third eye and circlet were drawn for Court and are CC0.
